### Justifications 
- Motor propeller must be matched according to motor's KV rating, battery voltage and recommended propeller size. We can check available thrust by using a thrust stand. 
- ESC rating must be at 25% more continuous current that the expected maximum motor current. BEC must also supply sufficient voltage and current for servo and receiver. 
- 11.1 V LiPo is suitable for starting option for a compatible brushless motor. 1500mAh will also be lighter than a 2200 mAh battery. 
- 4 servos , 2 for ailerons , one for elevator and one for rudder. The servos must have adequate stall torque rating. 
- Structural material has already been discussed in Notes section.

## Budget 
- Total budget comes out to be around 11,000 rupees. 
- Using foam board and wooden spars , reusing compatible tools and equipment , saving money on finishes is logical. 
- We must never compromise on battery safety , structural strength and electrical ratings.
